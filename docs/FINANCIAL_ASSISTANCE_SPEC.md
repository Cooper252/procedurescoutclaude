# Financial assistance spec

Showing possible assistance inside the shopping flow. Phase 4.

## Principles

- Per-facility policy records: URL, plain-language summary, effective dates, income thresholds as % of poverty guidelines, family-size rules, service and residency limits, discounts if officially stated, how to apply, last review date.
- Poverty guidelines stored per year from HHS/ASPE; the legacy hard-coded 2025 table is not reused.
- Never states eligibility; shows "you may qualify" with the policy's own terms.
- Not stacked with insurance benefits or other programs unless verified.
