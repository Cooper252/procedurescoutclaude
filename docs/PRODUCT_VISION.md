# Product vision

ProcedureScout turns hospital-published price files into plain answers to three questions: **what do you need, where, and how will you pay?**

## What it is

- A national hospital pricing data platform (discover → download → validate → parse → normalize → publish), with full lineage from every displayed number back to the source file and row.
- A free consumer comparison tool, no account required, that keeps four amounts separate: **gross charge**, **discounted cash price**, **payer-specific negotiated rate**, and **estimated patient cost** (derived, never reported).
- An honest explainer: every price shows its source, date, payer/plan, code and match type; every gap shows why it is a gap.

## What it is not

- Not a guarantee of what a patient will pay, and not an insurer adjudication.
- Not a quality ranking or a clinical recommendation engine.
- Not a place where AI generates prices, codes or eligibility decisions.

## Scope

Nevada first, designed for all 50 states and territories where data exists. Hospitals first; ambulatory surgery centers are listed with their different (and often absent) publication obligations made clear.

## Ultimate standard

A person can open ProcedureScout without registering, enter a location, choose how they pay, pick their carrier and plan, search a procedure in everyday words, compare real published prices, understand the price types, estimate their own share, see likely extra costs, check financial assistance, see source and date, understand what is unknown, and then verify with their insurer and provider.

## Governing rule

No price is better than a false price.
